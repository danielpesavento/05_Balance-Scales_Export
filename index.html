8.EE.7 Balance Scales 5: Expert - Knowledge Check - Interactive Practice

Every scale is balanced, and the triangle is half of the square and the bowl is half of the circle. In this set, weights can be decimals or fractions. Type a decimal (like 2.5) or a fraction (like 1/3). Answers within 0.01 are accepted. Check the shape key, study the worked example, then solve the 10 problems. For each problem, first type the weight of one shape, then type the number that goes in place of the ?. Every answer is a number (a decimal or a fraction is fine). You can answer in any order, and you can come back and submit again after you answer more problems. When you submit, answered problems are marked right or wrong and locked.
